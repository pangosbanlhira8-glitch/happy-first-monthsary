My Letter for You

To my babi, Hi, baby! I just want to take a moment to remind you how much you mean to me and how thankful I am to have someone like you in my life. I know sometimes I can be makulit, stubborn, and even a little bossy, but I hope you know that behind all of that is someone who genuinely loves you and wants nothing but the best for you. One of the things I love most about you is your soft heart. You’re so understanding, loving, and patient with me. Kahit minsan naiinis ka sa akin, you still find a way to forgive me and make things okay again. I appreciate that more than you know. Thank you for always listening to all my random stories, even those nonsense things na bigla ko na lang naiisip and yap about. HWHAHA. I love how I can talk to you about almost anything without feeling like I’m being annoying. You make me feel heard, appreciated, and loved even through the simplest things. And baby, please know that I really appreciate how you consider my feelings whenever you make decisions. Alam kong minsan strict ako and may mga bagay akong pinagbabawal sa’yo, but I hope you understand that it’s never because I want to control your life. I just care about you, your safety, and your future. I don’t want you getting into situations that could hurt you or affect the things you’re working hard for. I know you have your own decisions to make, and I respect that. I just hope we can always understand each other and consider one another’s feelings. Thank you for being so considerate of me. I’m also really proud of you for choosing Maritime and for taking another step toward your dreams. I know next school year will be different because you’ll be away for almost a year, and honestly, the thought of being in an LDR makes me a little emotional. I’ll miss your presence, our conversations, and all the little things that we usually get to share. There will probably be days when I wish I could just see you instead of waiting for your replies or calls. But I don’t want you to feel like you have to choose between loving me and pursuing your dreams. I want you to grow, learn, and become the person you’ve always wanted to be. I want to be someone who supports you while you’re building your future. Please don’t ever think that distance will make me less proud of you or less appreciative of everything you do. I hope we continue choosing each other even on the days when communication gets difficult or when our schedules don’t match. Let’s always make time for each other, communicate properly, and never let misunderstandings become bigger than our love and respect for one another. And lastly, babi, thank you for being you. Thank you for your patience, your efforts, your love, and even for tolerating my endless yapping. I may not always express it perfectly, but I notice the little things you do, and they mean so much to me. I hope you never get tired of being soft-hearted and loving, but please remember to take care of yourself, too. You deserve the same love, patience, and understanding that you always give to others. I love you so much, babi. I’m proud of you, I’m rooting for you, and I’ll always be happy to see you achieve the things you’ve been working hard for. No matter how busy life gets or how far your dreams take you, I hope we never forget why we started and why we’re still choosing each other. Always remember that I love you, babi. Take care of yourself always, okay? 🤍
